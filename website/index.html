01 — Why Flow

A good decision
shouldn’t get lost
in a good conversation.

Flow works where the conversation happens. It connects your team’s context, surfaces the signal, and waits for the human “yes” before anything moves.

02 — The flow

Less chasing.
More momentum.

  1. 01

    Listen

    It reads before it asks.

    Flow follows the thread, project docs, and product signals so your team doesn’t have to repeat itself.
  2. 02

    Clarify

    It makes the call visible.

    Options, evidence, owners, and the actual open question return to the same place the work started.
  3. 03

    Move

    It waits for approval.

    When the team agrees, Flow can create the issue, update the knowledge, or prepare a draft pull request.
